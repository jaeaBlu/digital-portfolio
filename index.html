About Britney

Designing for humans, engineering with intention.

A deep dive into my background, technical foundation, and design philosophy.

"Technology is at its best when it feels naturally intuitive, visually calm, and genuinely accessible to everyone."

I am a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology graduate. My work is shaped by a dual passion: the logical discipline of clean, accessible code, paired with an eye for thoughtful layout, typography, and visual simplicity.

Rather than viewing software engineering and visual design as separate disciplines, I unite them. In every production application, user interface system, or branding project, I champion human-centered empathy, WCAG accessibility, semantic code quality, and responsive layout fidelity.

Equipped with comprehensive training in frontend development (HTML5, CSS3, modern JavaScript), Python, Figma prototyping, and multimedia production, I am prepared to build purposeful digital products that deliver measurable real-world impact.
